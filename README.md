# I Am an Heir — for Grown-ups 👑

**Daily Declarations for Grown-ups** — speak the Word of God over your own life, out loud, every day.

> *"...heirs of God, and joint-heirs with Christ."* — Romans 8:17 (KJV)
> *"For as he thinketh in his heart, so is he."* — Proverbs 23:7 (KJV)

## What it is

A voice-first daily app where you:

- hear **today's declaration** from a perennial 365-day calendar (same word, same date, every year),
- **declare it** three times so the Word takes root, with a growing daily streak,
- tap **What do I need today?** to stand on God's Word for exactly where you are — *fear, finances, marriage, children, work, healing, weariness, a big decision, grief, spiritual attack, waiting, purpose,* and more,
- declare **Who I Am in Christ**.

All **386 declarations** are drawn from and verified against the King James Version of the Holy Bible, across 22 areas of life.

## How to use

Open the app, add yourself (and your spouse, if you like — each keeps their own daily walk), and declare together. Installable on phones and tablets ("Add to Home Screen"), and works offline.
